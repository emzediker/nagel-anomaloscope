What the student is doing:
  1. Adjust the mixture field until the upper field appears yellow like the lower field.
  2. Adjust the test-field radiance until the brightness also matches.
  3. For a typical normal observer, the representative match is approximately 45 / 17.

This is a simplified screen-based teaching model. A real Nagel anomaloscope uses spectrally narrow 546-nm green, 670-nm red, and approximately 589–590-nm yellow stimuli with calibrated luminance. A tablet display cannot reproduce those physical stimuli exactly.
